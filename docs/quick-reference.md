# Quick Reference — One Page

Print or bookmark this. Full explanations live in the chapters.

## The tools in one breath

- **Five-Minute Doorway** ([ch. 2](02-five-minute-doorway.md)) — touch the
  task for one 2-second doorway action; run a five-minute timer on top; both
  stopping and continuing at the buzzer count as wins.
- **Body doubling** ([ch. 3](03-body-doubling.md)) — no advice, just presence:
  "be near me while I do my own thing."
- **Promise timer** ([ch. 4](04-timers.md)) — 5 minutes, always paired with a
  doorway. Stopping at zero is the deal working, not failing.
- **Defiance timer** ([ch. 4](04-timers.md)) — 10 minutes, brain-dump every
  possible step, throw away the junk.
- **Switch timer** ([ch. 4](04-timers.md)) — set it when you start, catch
  hyperfocus and tab-drift at the boundary.
- **Progress card** ([ch. 5](05-progress-cards.md)) — one visible sheet, one
  mark per doorway or session, never erased, no streaks.
- **AI shrinker** ([ch. 6](06-ai-nudges.md)) — "5 first steps, under 2
  minutes, physical, no plans. even smaller."
- **The ladder** ([ch. 7](07-stuck-task-ladder.md)) — when nothing above
  mobilizes: climb one rung at a time from the printed card.

## The AI prompts, verbatim

```
Shrinker:  I'm stuck on [task]. 5 possible FIRST STEPS, each under 2
           minutes and physical. No plans, no advice. Then say "even smaller."

Declumper: Sort this dump into: not-actually-needed / not-mine / 5-minute
           actions / one real next action today: [dump everything]

Hype-lamp: I have to [task] and I keep avoiding it. One true encouraging
           sentence, then the smallest possible first physical action,
           then remind me I can stop after that.
```

## The three sentences (emergency yardage)

1. *"Starting is a separate job from doing."*
2. *"Starting doesn't trap me — I can stop at the buzzer."*
3. *"This doesn't have to be good. It has to be started."*

## When nothing works at all

That's a body-and-basics day: water, food, light, a different room, and
permission to try again after a meal. Rung 1 of the ladder is a legitimate
way to spend a day. Zero marks on the card don't erase the ones that are
already there.
