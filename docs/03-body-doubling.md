# Chapter 3 — Body Doubling

Some brains can only press "start" while another human is in the room.
That's not a crutch. That's a nervous system function.

## What it is

**Body doubling** = working on your own task, at your own pace, alongside
another person who is working on their own thing. They don't help. They don't
advise. They just *exist, working nearby*, and your brain quietly borrows
their ignition.

It works weirdly well for ADHD brains: another person's focused presence acts
as a gentle external rhythm section. You don't have to generate your own
beat—your brain syncs to theirs by default.

## Ways to do it, cheapest first

1. **Ambient presence** — another person in the house doing their own chores
   or reading. Announce it: "I'm doing my taxes over here till 2:00." No
   schedule agreement needed; coexisting counts.
2. **The declared check-in** — same, but you tell them precisely what the
   doorway is and when you'll report back ("folder found by 2"). External
   accountability is a turbo setting; use when Presence Alone fails.
3. **Virtual doubles** — webcam services such as Flow Club, Focusmate etc.,
   Discord or other servers with a focused-work channel, or any friend
   staying on a voice call while you both do your thing. Silence is fine on
   the call; shuffling, typing sounds OK too.
4. **Asynchronous doubling** — a friend sends you "starting now?" and you
   text back "doorway done" an hour later. Weaker but real.
5. **Recorded presence** — study-with-me videos, "body doubling" ambience
   recordings. The lowest fidelity, and still occasionally enough.

## Rules that make it not-sucky

- **No recruiting for advice.** You are not asking for help with the task.
  You are asking: *"Will you just be near me while I do my thing?"*
- **You're allowed to leave early.** Heaven's sake, yes. Early-and-done is the
  system working, not a failure to maximize.
- **No comparing.** If your double is doing their whole CRM migration and you
  found one folder of receipts, you both succeeded. Different rooms.
- **A mess is enough.** If finding a human to co-work with feels like a task
  in itself, that's a rung-one/ladder-problem. Ladder first, doubling later.

## Script for asking a friend

Copy, edit, send:

> "Hey — no advice or help needed. I'm doing this boring thing I've been
> stuck on, and my brain starts WAY more easily with someone else around.
> Can you be on a call / in the room doing your own stuff from 2:00 to 2:40?
> You can hang up whenever. This is a co-working thing, not a favor I'll owe."

Most people get it instantly — lots of them have the same problem.
